*[AWSOME]: Avalanche Warning Service Operational Meteo Environment: the open-source snow cover framework
*[HRDPS]: High Resolution Deterministic Prediction System: ECCC/MSC's 2.5 km weather model
*[MSC]: Meteorological Service of Canada
*[ECCC]: Environment and Climate Change Canada
*[BTL]: Below treeline
*[TL]: Treeline
*[ALP]: Alpine
*[Sk38]: Skier stability index for a 38° slope. Below 1 = unstable
*[Sk38_CCL]: Physically based stability index: critical cut length ÷ length of the crack a skier creates. Below 1 = unstable
*[CCL]: Critical cut length: how easily a crack spreads in the weak layer
*[Punstable]: Machine-learning score (0–1, Mayer et al. 2022): how closely a layer resembles unstable layers in Swiss field tests
*[Pwet]: Machine-learning score (0–1, Hendrick et al. 2023): how likely a day is a wet-snow avalanche day
*[LWC]: Liquid water content
*[PWL]: Persistent weak layer (SH, DH, FC, FCxr)
*[RTA]: Relative threshold sum approach: structural weak-layer index
*[HS]: Snow height
*[SWE]: Snow water equivalent
*[UTC]: Coordinated Universal Time. 06:00 UTC = 22:00 Pacific (PST) the evening before
*[MWHS]: Mike Wiegele Heliskiing
*[DEM]: Digital elevation model
*[MRDEM]: Medium Resolution Digital Elevation Model (NRCan, 30 m)
*[SMET]: Text format for SNOWPACK weather input
*[WEonG]: Weather Elements on Grid: MSC's post-processed HRDPS fields
